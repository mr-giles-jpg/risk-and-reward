Recording your progress
  • Where available, use Save & Exit to download your game save (.json)
  • Move the .json file to your school-approved OneDrive folder
  • When instructed, export your investment report as PDF for submission, if the browser permits
  • If saving or printing is blocked, use the Performance worksheet tables to copy your net worth, decisions, news and final financial results onto your paper worksheet
  • If returning mid-game, reopen the relevant part and choose Load saved game

V6 keeps browser progress separate from V5.9. If you were using the earlier game, save a JSON backup before switching.

This educational game uses fictional investments and simulated prices. Game progress is saved in your browser when supported. This website does not automatically send your answers to your teacher.
